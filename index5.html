How to Play:

  • Watch the animation: first line appears, then the intersecting line grows from one end
  • Estimate what percentage along the black line the intersection occurs
  • Enter your guess as a whole number between 0 and 100
  • Both ends are acceptable (15% and 85% are equivalent)
  • Get 3 points for an exact match
  • Get 1 point for guesses within 10% of the actual value
  • Build your streak with consecutive correct answers!
